Research

From a research gap to a testable hypothesis

This page sets out the scientific foundation of APTIVRA: the research gap we start from, the hypothesis we test, and the field protocol that turns it into evidence.

The Research Gap

Most research measures adoption, not impact

Plenty of studies today track how often AI tools are used inside organizations, but few measure with precision whether that use actually translates into measurable improvement in workplace performance. APTIVRA sets out to close exactly this gap — through a field research design, not perception surveys alone.

Testable

Core Hypothesis

Structured training on AI tools, when applied within a real work context, is expected to improve measurable performance quality and speed — compared to mere theoretical familiarity with the tool.

Field Protocol

Five stages, from baseline to evidence

01

Baseline

Documenting performance and digital capability levels before any intervention, to establish an accurate reference point.

02

Intervention

A training program focused on specific AI tools, tied directly to participants' actual tasks.

03

Workplace Application

A field observation period for how participants actually use the tools within their daily work.

04

Post-Assessment

Re-measurement using the same baseline instruments to determine the actual size of change.

05

Evidence

Analysis and documentation of results in a report ready for academic review and scientific publication.

Measurement Framework

Three dimensions, not a single number

The protocol measures impact across three complementary dimensions, rather than settling for one general indicator that misses the reality of the workplace.

Capability

AI literacy, effective use of the tool, and responsible behaviour toward it.

Performance

Task completion time and actual output quality.

Adaptability

Confidence in using the tool, capacity to adapt, and the employee's own sense of future employability.

Why Now?

AI adoption inside organizations is accelerating faster than their ability to measure whether it is used effectively and responsibly — a gap that widens with every new wave of adoption.

Why Morocco?

An emerging economy that represents a meaningful test context, on a pathway that extends from Morocco to North Africa and the Global South.
