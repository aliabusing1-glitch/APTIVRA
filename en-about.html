About

An independent research initiative, fully transparent about its nature

APTIVRA is not an institution or a consulting firm — it is an independent research initiative led by a single researcher, seeking academic and institutional partnerships that give it the scientific rigor it deserves.

The Lead

Lahcen Afaadas

An independent researcher from Morocco, developing APTIVRA alongside a parallel project titled "AI for Employees" — a practical, Arabic-language guide to workplace AI readiness. Both projects share the same core question: how learning to use AI can translate into real, measurable workplace capability.

Institutional Note

APTIVRA, at its current stage, is an individual research initiative, not affiliated with any university or institution. Building a team is presented only as a possible future step, should the initiative grow through genuine partnerships. This transparency is a core part of the project's credibility with any academic or institutional partner.

Why This Path?

From a field observation to a research protocol

APTIVRA began from a simple observation: most AI training programs measure participation or satisfaction, not actual impact on performance. From there came the idea of building a short, repeatable field protocol — one that could first be tested in Morocco, then expanded through genuine academic and institutional partnerships.
