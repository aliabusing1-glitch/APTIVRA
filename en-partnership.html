Partnership Model

We are looking for the right research partner, not a funder

APTIVRA is designed as a three-partner model, combining academic rigor, local institutional context, and the leadership of the initiative itself.

The Three-Partner Model

Academic / Research Partner

A university or academic researcher who provides methodological oversight, reviews the research design, and connects the findings to existing literature on workforce readiness.

Local Institutional Partner

An entity in Morocco (a company, a training body, or an educational institution) that provides access to the field participant pool and a genuine work context.

APTIVRA

Protocol design, field coordination, and documentation of results within a transparent, reviewable research framework.

What We Are Seeking

Forms of collaboration on the table

Academic Level

Scientific review of the protocol design, joint supervision of the analysis, or co-publication of results.

Institutional Level

Providing a field participant cohort, or hosting the first trial within a genuine work environment.

Funding pathways may be explored jointly, subject to partner fit and eligibility.
